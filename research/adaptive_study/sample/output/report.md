## Research prerequisites
- Pre-register hypothesis, primary outcome, analysis plan and duration before first participant.
- Confirm ethics/guardian consent, assignment integrity and independent measurement.
- Preserve all assigned participants and report missing outcomes / attrition (intention-to-treat).
- Prevent policy cross-over and intervention contamination.
- Include uncertainty intervals and sample-size justification before efficacy claims.
- Do not publish participant-level study logs to the public website.

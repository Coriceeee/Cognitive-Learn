# Data Card — VNHSGE (Hugging Face)

- **HF dataset:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/roshansk23/Vietnam_HighSchool_Exam_Dataset
- **Original project:** https://github.com/Xdao85/VNHSGE
- **Paper:** arXiv:2305.12199.
- **HF card metadata:** generic `cc`; exact Creative Commons variant not stated in the card currently reviewed.
- **HF viewer:** 6,663 multiple-choice question rows across eight subjects.
- **Potential use:** Vietnamese high-school exam question-answering and LLM knowledge benchmark after license/source rights are clarified.
- **Not suitable for:** Student-level outcome prediction, longitudinal learning analytics, SCI/MAS/CSL validation or adaptive intervention effectiveness.
- **Decision:** HOLD for redistribution/training until exact rights are resolved. Retain only as a reviewed candidate in the source register.

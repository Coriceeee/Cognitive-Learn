# CLA — CP42 Holistic & Temporal Intelligence

Base: `CLA_FULL_SOURCE_CP42_ROBUST_DECISION_20261118.zip`. Retains Student-only, chart-first, National Admission and all prior engine behavior. No rules, admissions records, model training or live AI changes.

## Implemented (local, advisory)
- **Lumen Temporal Diagnostics:** `src/lib/temporalDiagnostics.ts` computes median pairwise slopes per elapsed week (Theil–Sen); a candidate two-segment median/L1 split needs at least three distinct observations and seven elapsed days per side, an absolute median shift of at least the existing metric threshold, and a loss reduction of at least 50%. Choose the largest qualifying reduction, earliest if exactly tied. Latest observation is compared only against >=5 preceding observations spanning >=7 days; descriptive threshold max(existing metric threshold, 3*1.4826*MAD). Metric thresholds 3 hours or 15 scale points; slope direction deadband is half that threshold per week. Use only already accepted contiguous recent series; no p-values, clinical interpretation, calibrated uncertainty, autocorrelation adjustment or causal effect. Candidate split and outlier flags are distinct and can overlap. Latest save per UTC day; up to ten snapshots.
- **Lumen Holistic Roadmap:** `src/lib/holisticRoadmap.ts` and `LumenAdaptiveRoadmap.tsx` use the server-confirmed Seed and its verified history from the existing session hook. Student supplies horizon 1–104 weeks and optional goal reconsideration, financial aid, study-abroad interests. Candidate behavior changes prompt review only, not intervention. Timeline links to existing Atlas, Orion, Pulse, Haven and Seed. Does not set official deadlines or alter profile, policy or cycle. Actions are a review agenda, not a complete execution planner. Half-time and final-week milestones are design choices. Options unsaved and local only.
- **Haven Annual Budget Scenarios:** `src/lib/annualBudgetScenario.ts` and `HavenBudgetScenario.tsx` compare gross tuition/living/other, confirmed aid, optional potential aid and stress scenario. All monetary fields explicitly entered for the same annual period in integer VND. Confirmed-only, conditional aid and stress are separate, stress increases non-tuition costs by user-selected %. Tuition cap separately checks gross annual tuition. Missing entries do not become zero; no verification of awards, exchange rates or actual costs. No persistence, transmission or decision automation. No implicit import of Seed budget whose time basis may differ.

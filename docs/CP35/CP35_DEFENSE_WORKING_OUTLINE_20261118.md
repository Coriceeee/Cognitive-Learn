## Hard questions / answers
- Is SCI/MAS/CSL a validated psychological scale? No, experimental composite indices; repeated-measure and construct evidence needed.
- Why 60% / 75%? Prespecified engineering threshold for optional workload advice only; sensitivity and empirical evaluation pending.
- Does an adaptive policy prove improvement? No. Technical divergence and selectable draft do not establish assignment or comparative efficacy.
- What if the next plan is unsafe or too demanding? No automatic increase, cap to prior workload and observed hours on lower-adherence branch, human choice/manual review. These are workload guardrails, not clinical safety validation.
- Why not rely on Gemini for everything? It retrieves and explains when authorized; engines compute scores, decisions and provenance. It must not fabricate probabilities or facts.
- Is 848/848 accuracy? No. Consistency with existing Atlas data only; no national coverage or prospective accuracy claim.
- Is 120/120 LLM groundedness? No. Author-created deterministic retrieval cases; live model evaluation pending.
- Why UCI? External method feasibility; Portuguese schools, no direct THPT inference, no known true participant IDs/time split.
- What proves protection? Candidate rules + local static tests only; Emulator and deployed permissions still must be tested.
- Can you reproduce the results? Local scripts and fingerprint verified; missing real dataset/model/prod run prevents universal claim.
- What did the student implement? Maintain contribution and AI usage logs with actual evidence; no fabricated attribution. Student must demonstrate and explain decisions.
- What if ML does not beat baseline? Report negative result; no model promotion on weak evidence.

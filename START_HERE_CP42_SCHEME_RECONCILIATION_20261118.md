- Coverage separates any combination evidence, at least one mappable combination, and at least one conditional/unmapped combination. These are not all mutually exclusive metrics; the new stacked chart uses three disjoint groups (mappable, source-only, no source). School/year queue includes partial mapping/condition gaps.
